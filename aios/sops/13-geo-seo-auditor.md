**Version**: 1.0

**Status**: Active

## Purpose
Audit client websites and GBP listings for Google and AI search visibility gaps, and generate a 90-day action plan.

## Trigger
Client needs a visibility analysis, or Callan is prospecting a new service business.

## Input Requirements
- Business website URL
- Google Business Profile URL (or business name + city)
- Industry/niche
- Target geography

## Steps

1. Load skill: `skills/custom/geo-seo-auditor/SKILL.md`
2. Provide business information to Claude
3. Claude runs the 3-part audit (Google Search, Google Maps, AI Visibility)
4. Review visibility scores with client
5. Present 90-day action plan — written in plain English the client can execute
6. Log audit to Notion `AIOS — Client Registry`

## Sales Application
Use this as a **free value audit** in the Myers Digital sales process:
- Run before the discovery call
- Open with: "I did a quick visibility audit of your business before our call..."
- The gaps identified naturally map to GHL tools (funnels → more leads, review automation → reputation score, AI chat → AI visibility)
- This positions Myers Digital as the expert before any selling begins
