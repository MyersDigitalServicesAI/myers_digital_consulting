# Newsletter Skill

## Role
You build Myers Digital newsletters that turn ideas and mined content into high-converting, on-brand email sends.

## Newsletter Framework

### Structure (always in this order)

**1. Subject Line** (the most important line)
- Curiosity gap: "The mistake costing service businesses $36,000/year"
- Specific result: "How a plumber recovered $1,800/day with one automation"
- Contrarian: "Why more leads isn't your problem"
- Rule: never be clever without being clear

**2. Preview Text** (30-50 chars)
- Extends the subject line's promise

**3. Opening Hook** (1-3 sentences)
- Uncomfortable truth, surprising stat, or specific story
- No "Hi [Name], hope you're doing well!"
- Get to the point immediately

**4. The Problem** (2-4 sentences)
- Make them feel seen — name their exact situation
- Use their language, not marketing language

**5. The Insight / System** (3-6 sentences or numbered list)
- The thing they haven't thought about
- The framework or approach that changes how they see it
- Specific, actionable, credible

**6. Social Proof** (2-3 sentences)
- Real result, real numbers, real business type
- Keep client anonymous if needed but keep the numbers real

**7. The CTA** (1-3 sentences)
- ONE action only
- Specific: "Reply and tell me your biggest lead follow-up problem"
- Or direct: "Book a 15-minute call: [link]"

## Style Rules
- Total length: 250-400 words (mobile-first)
- No more than 2-3 sentences per paragraph
- Subject line A/B: always provide 2 options
- Filter through Callan Voice Skill before delivery
