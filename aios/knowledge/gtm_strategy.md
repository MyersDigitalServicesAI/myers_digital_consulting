## Platform Strategy

### LinkedIn (Primary — Agencies & Resellers)
- Post cadence: 4–5x/week
- Format mix: Text-only long-form (highest reach), short punchy takes, 1-image posts
- Goal: Build authority as the AI ops expert, drive demo bookings via DM
- Engage in comments on posts by GHL creators, AI marketing accounts, agency growth voices

### Facebook Groups (Secondary — GHL Community)
- Target groups: GoHighLevel Official, HighLevel Explained, GHL Agency Owners, Skool communities
- Post cadence: 2–3x/week, value-first, no direct pitching in posts
- Strategy: Answer questions, share insights, DM people who engage with your posts

### Instagram (Supporting — Brand Awareness)
- Short-form carousel breakdowns, behind-the-scenes, agent demos
- Links back to demo booking in bio

## Launch Phases

### Phase 1 — Plant the Problem (Weeks 1–2)
**Goal:** Get buyers to recognize themselves in the bottleneck story
**Content:** Problem-aware posts, no product mention
**Action:** Start following and engaging with 20 target accounts daily

### Phase 2 — Show the System (Weeks 3–4)
**Goal:** Introduce AIOS as the answer to the problem raised in Phase 1
**Content:** How it works, the 24 agents, the math, behind-the-scenes
**Action:** DM everyone who commented/liked Phase 1 posts — "I built something that solves exactly this"

### Phase 3 — Create Urgency (Weeks 5–6)
**Goal:** Convert warm leads into founding clients
**Content:** Founding pricing posts, countdown, direct offer
**Action:** Personal outreach to every engaged follower with a specific, relevant demo offer

### Post 6 — THE THREE LAYERS (LinkedIn/Instagram | Week 3)
**Pillar:** The System
**Goal:** Explain the architecture simply, build confidence in the product

---

AIOS runs on three layers. Here's how they fit together:

**Layer 1: The Brain**
24 AI agents built on Anthropic's Claude.
One Director Agent routes every task to the right specialist.
The specialists execute — CRM, Finance, Marketing, Operations, Analytics, and more.

**Layer 2: The Memory**
Notion acts as the long-term memory.
8 databases track everything: client registry, decisions made, KPIs over time, SOPs, automation logs.
Every agent reads from it before acting. Every significant decision gets written back.
Nothing gets lost between sessions.

**Layer 3: The Automation**
Zapier connects the AI to your real tools.
25 webhooks fire automatically — GHL pipeline updates, invoice triggers, lead alerts, report distribution.
The agents think. Zapier executes. Your tools update.

No layer works without the other two.
That's the difference between an AI tool and an operating system.
