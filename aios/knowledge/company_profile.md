## Core Platform

**GoHighLevel (GHL)** is the primary technology platform. Myers Digital runs as a GHL Agency with sub-accounts provisioned per client. AIOS is built on top of GHL + Claude AI + Zapier + Notion.

---

## Ideal Client Profile (ICP)

**Best fit:**
- Service business generating $20K–$200K/month
- Owner spending 3+ hours/day on operations, reporting, or content
- Has GHL or is open to it as their primary platform
- Values systems over grinding
- Roofing, plumbing, HVAC, landscaping, home services

**Poor fit:**
- Under $10K/month revenue
- Wants to DIY everything
- No marketing spend or growth intent
- Wants a VA, not an AI operating system

---

## Core Pain Points Solved

1. **Missed calls** → lost revenue (avg $600/missed call for home services)
2. **Zero follow-up system** → dead leads
3. **Manual reporting** → 10–15 hours/week wasted
4. **No content engine** → invisible online
5. **Ops chaos** → owner-dependent business that can't scale
